Notes

Criteria definitions

Use of IBM Bob
How meaningfully IBM Bob was used to plan, build, test or explain the solution, and how well the team can describe what Bob contributed.
Solution Completeness
How much of the solution works from start to finish. The main features run in a demo and address the stated problem.
Problem Market / Persona Fit
How clearly the team identifies a real problem and who has it, and how well the solution meets that person's needs.
Creativity
How original the idea or approach is, including a fresh take on a familiar problem or an inventive use of the tools.
Presentation
How clearly and confidently the team explains the problem, demonstrates the solution, keeps to time and answers questions.
Teamwork
How well the team shared the work, collaborated and involved every member in building or presenting.
